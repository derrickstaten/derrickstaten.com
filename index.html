Track record

Two products built from zero as technical cofounder, and eight years at Branch Metrics helping grow it from no revenue to $100M ARR.

Sightline OS

Technical cofounder · 2025–26

I built the entire product solo: a Python data-ingestion pipeline on AWS, Postgres storage, and a Next.js web app that turns messy supply-chain data into insights for restaurant teams. Sightline's customers include national operators like Din Tai Fung and Bonchon. I built it over a year, then hired and onboarded the engineers who run it now.

Branch Metrics

Early engineer → head of product · 2015–23

I joined Branch pre-Series A as one of the first few engineers and stayed eight years: two in engineering on core product and Branch's first paid products, then six in product, eventually running the team. I led the Facebook attribution integration that anchored the ads business and became the template for the Google, Snap, and TikTok integrations after it. Branch went from no revenue to $100M ARR, and from a handful of people to 500+, while I was there.

The League

Technical cofounder · 2014

As the sole engineer, I built the entire product end to end: the iOS app and the LinkedIn/Facebook screening engine that became the app's signature. We launched in San Francisco in 2014 to the first thousands of users. The app was later acquired by Match Group for ~$30M.
