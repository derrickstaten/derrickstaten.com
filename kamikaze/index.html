Kamikaze Fighter

Controls

  • ←↑↓→Move
  • SpaceFire
  • 1–5Switch gun
  • PPause
  • CContinue
  • EscQuit

Rules

Gun upgrades unlocked at 5, 10, 15 and 20 kills. Extra lives at 30, 60 and 90. Take a hit and the streak resets — you lose every gun back down to the first. Gun 4 fires a three-way spread; gun 5 is “guided”. Note: enemies are fully invisible on the final level, level 13; that’s part of the fun.

About

Written in QBASIC in 2004 by Derrick Staten on a school lab machine. A single loop detects input (ship movement, firing guns) and moves enemies. Levels were loaded via CSV, five guns, ASCII character 127 (⌂) for a ship, leaderboard stored in plain text. Classmates had fun manually writing themselves into the top of the leaderboard file.

Rewritten by Claude in Javascript, staying faithful to all of the quirks of the original.
